# V0.8 — Reference integration and audit

Two user-supplied reference frames were inspected visually. This document records only visible properties; it does not infer hidden production methods, 3D topology, or the original artists' intent.

## Reference 01 — close-up anime eye

Visible properties:
- both eyes share a strongly similar outer construction;
- large golden/yellow irises with orange/brown variation toward the center and lower area;
- bright white specular/highlight elements inside the eye;
- heavy dark upper lash/eyelid line;
- visible lower lashes with a reddish accent;
- dark purple hair framing the eyes;
- very high local contrast between dark contour and bright eye interior;
- gaze is not perfectly centered and reads as directed toward the side/front depending on eye geometry.

Engine implications:
- pair-level morphology is required;
- multiple structured highlight objects are required;
- iris color must support layered color, ring and pattern structure;
- eyelid/lash system must be independent from iris/pupil;
- the default outline contract is black.

## Reference 02 — enlarged reflective eye

Visible properties:
- a single very large eye fills most of the frame;
- the visible eyeball has a rounded/curved appearance rather than a flat icon-like almond;
- a scene is visibly reflected inside the optical surface;
- there is a bright broad reflection crossing the surface;
- the lower/inner eye area carries yellow-green illumination;
- the surrounding eye contour is very dark;
- the reflected content is itself visually structured and contains multiple figures.

Engine implications:
- reflection must be represented as a structured layer, not a single hard-coded white dot;
- a future reflection compositor must be able to project another vector scene into an eye surface;
- shot scale is a composition property, not an eye morphology property;
- a huge close-up can reuse exactly the same eye definition as a normal shot.

## Explicit non-claims

The frames do not prove any particular anatomical, rendering, shader, or 3D implementation. V0.8 therefore adds a representation model for these visible behaviors without claiming to reproduce the original source rendering process.

## Pair invariant introduced in V0.8

For a human-default pair, left/right eyes share the same structural definition by default: outer shape family, iris/pupil ratios, lid-curve controls and contour variation. Small size asymmetry is permitted through an explicit policy. Independent appearance is permitted so legitimate differences such as heterochromia do not require a structural mismatch.

For non-human/fantasy use cases, `EyePairPolicy(mode="free")` removes the bilateral structural restriction.
