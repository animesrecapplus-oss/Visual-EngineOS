# Visual Engine V1.0 — Ocular Core

V1.0 is a hardening release of the eye subsystem. It does not replace the bilateral model established in V0.7–V0.9.

## 1. Human eye is a pair

`EyePairSpec` remains the preferred human entry point.

Default contract:

- both eyes share the same morphology definition;
- controlled bilateral size/rotation asymmetry is allowed;
- appearance may differ explicitly, e.g. heterochromia;
- non-human/free mode may diverge structurally;
- expression intent is shared by the pair unless explicitly overridden at a higher entity layer.

A pair is therefore a semantic unit, not two unrelated render calls.

## 2. Eyelid is not the aperture outline

V0.9 had upper/lower lid margins but the reference render exposed a weakness: those lines could visually disappear into the generic eye contour.

V1.0 separates:

```text
Aperture boundary / lid margin
        +
Visible eyelid fold
```

`EyeGeometry` now carries:

- `upper_lid_points`
- `lower_lid_points`
- `upper_crease_points`
- `lower_crease_points`
- canthus anchors

The renderer draws the folds independently. This makes the mandatory eyelid perceptible in a standalone eye render.

## 3. Upper and lower lashes are separate systems

Upper lashes are intentionally the dominant lash system:

- denser;
- longer;
- darker by default;
- strongly stylizable;
- outward/upward corner flare.

Lower lashes are a separate `LowerLashSpec`:

- fewer;
- shorter;
- thinner;
- lower opacity;
- default neutral gray rather than hard black;
- subtle outward/downward direction.

This is a visual authoring contract, not a biological universal. Entity/style overrides remain possible.

## 4. Lash geometry follows the actual lid

Lashes are sampled from the corresponding upper/lower lid curve. They are not placed against a generic ellipse and do not use an unconstrained surface normal.

Upper direction:

- upward bias throughout;
- lateral flare increases toward the canthi;
- deterministic spacing;
- optional deterministic jitter;
- style controls change actual geometry.

Lower direction:

- predominantly downward;
- short and quiet;
- slight outward flare near the corners.

## 5. Eyebrow constraint

The eyebrow is a separate component and is constrained to the superior region of the eye.

The superior boundary now includes the upper eyelid fold, not only the aperture boundary. This prevents an eyebrow from being emitted between the lid margin and the lid fold.

## 6. Ocular expression

V1.0 introduces a renderer-independent `OcularExpressionSolver`.

```text
ExpressionIntent
        ↓
OcularExpressionSolver
        ↓
resolved EyeParameters + EyeAppearance
        ↓
Gaze + Eyelids + Geometry
        ↓
Renderer
```

Supported authoring intents:

- neutral
- joy
- sadness
- anger
- fear
- surprise
- pain
- stress
- sleepy
- crying

The profiles are bounded parameter deltas. They are not claims of biological truth and are not inferred from pixels.

## 7. Layer order

The intended dependency direction is:

```text
Identity / morphology
        ↓
Pair constraints
        ↓
Presentation profile
        ↓
Expression intent
        ↓
Gaze
        ↓
Eyelid configuration
        ↓
Ocular state
        ↓
Appearance / material
        ↓
Geometry
        ↓
Occlusion
        ↓
Renderer
```

The current code implements the eye-level parts of this chain. General scene occlusion and physical optical simulation remain outside V1.0.
