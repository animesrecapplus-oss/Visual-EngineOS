# V0.9 Reference Audit

## Reference 01 — anime golden pair

Observed and converted into engine requirements:

1. The two eyes behave as a pair, not as unrelated objects.
2. The pair can be visually asymmetric without losing shared identity.
3. The upper contour is the dominant lash-bearing boundary.
4. Lashes are dense enough to read as a continuous stylized family rather than isolated distant marks.
5. The lashes stay regular through most of the upper lid and change direction subtly toward the outer corner.
6. The lower boundary must return to the canthus without a triangular arrow/spike.
7. The iris can have a base color, inner/outer variation, radial pattern, rings and highlights.
8. Pupil, iris, sclera and contour have independent color contracts.
9. The eyebrow is a separate stylizable component located above the eye.

The reference is not used as a fixed template. Its visible characteristics become parameterized capabilities.

## Reference 02 — reflective large eye

Observed and converted into engine requirements:

1. A large/vertically elongated ocular presentation is valid.
2. A close-up/detail shot can make the eye occupy most of the frame.
3. The iris may expose only part of the sclera depending on size/opening.
4. A pupil must never visibly escape the aperture. Clipping is a rendering invariant, not a visual suggestion.
5. Highlights may be multiple and differently shaped.
6. A future reflection can contain scene geometry rather than a single white highlight.

V0.9 implements the first five items as structured vector capabilities. It does not claim to implement true physical scene reflection.
