## 8. Optical containment

Iris/pupil content remains clipped by the actual aperture. Gaze solving and explicit pupil offsets are constrained before rendering, while SVG clipping remains a final defensive boundary.

V1.0 does not claim physical corneal refraction or physically projected environment reflections.

## 9. Color and contour contract

Every visible eye component has independent appearance data. Defaults remain:

- contour: black;
- pupil: black;
- sclera/conjunctiva: white;
- upper lashes: black;
- lower lashes: subdued gray;
- iris: independently specified color;
- eyebrow: independently specified color.

Color and contour are not hard-coded into geometry.

## 10. Persistence

Migration `003_v1_0_ocular_system.sql` adds database profiles for:

- upper/lower lash systems;
- eyelid appearance profiles;
- ocular expression profiles.

PostgreSQL remains a source of truth, not a per-pixel runtime dependency.

## 11. Deliberate nonclaims

Still outside V1.0:

- full 3D eyeball anatomy;
- physical corneal refraction;
- projected scene reflection solver;
- muscle-level eyelid simulation;
- automatic expression inference from images;
- general face occlusion/depth solver;
- automatic reference-to-parameter fitting;
- biological tear dynamics;
- photorealistic iris fiber reconstruction.
