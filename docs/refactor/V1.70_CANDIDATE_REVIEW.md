# V1.70 candidate review — preserved architectural correction

## Candidate A — `resolve_component_contract`

Fresh V1.69 audit found the legacy wrapper at `runtime/contract.py:277-300` with real public/test consumers but no
production callers. A bounded extraction was prototyped and hostile graph audit exposed a new dependency cycle:
`runtime.contract ↔ runtime.resolve_component_contract` caused by the required legacy compatibility re-export.

The candidate was **not certified and was fully reverted**. No compatibility break or production semantic change was
accepted. This is recorded as an architectural correction, not as an implemented component.

`ERR-V170-CAND-001`: extracting the wrapper alone would require either a hidden dynamic dependency, a duplicate
resolver implementation, or a broader resolver extraction. All three violate the one-owner/no-hidden-coupling rule.

Decision: reopen impact and select a different proven micro-component rather than force the blocked extraction.
