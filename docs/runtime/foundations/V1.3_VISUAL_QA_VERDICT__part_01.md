# V1.3 — Visual QA verdict, corrections and remaining work

## Purpose

This document records a historical visual QA pass performed after the V1.3 state/effect checkpoint.
It is historical visual-QA evidence. A5 now restores the complete reproduction source and current
archive-integrity gate under `tools/scripts_visual_qa_v13_feedback.py`, `tools/verify_a5_visual_qa.py`
and `artifacts/history/v1.3/qa_feedback/manifest.json`.

The goal is not to declare the eye system "perfect", but to compare the actual rendered output with the intended visual contract and turn every discovered defect into either a correction, a test, or an explicit remaining-work item.

## Corrections made from visual inspection

### 1. Gaze / ocular assembly

The previous visual model could make the internal ocular elements appear to move independently. The corrected contract is:

```text
sclera / conjunctiva
        │
        └── ocular aperture
                │
                └── iris + pupil = one rigid gaze assembly
                              │
                              └── highlights follow the assembly
```

The pupil is the aperture in the iris. Therefore, in the normal anatomical mode, the pupil remains centered in the iris while the iris/pupil assembly moves across the visible eye aperture. Custom multi-pupil offsets remain possible as an explicit non-default authoring feature.

This is deliberately different from treating the iris as a fixed disk and sliding the pupil independently inside it.

### 2. Gaze-driven eyelid follow

A bounded renderer-independent coupling was added. Looking up/down now changes the eyelid geometry instead of leaving a completely rigid aperture around the moving internal assembly.

This is **not** yet the final anatomical eyelid solver. It is a controlled intermediate behaviour with explicit comments and tests.

### 3. Pupil containment

Custom pupil offsets are clamped after assembly placement as well. A large authoring offset cannot bypass the iris/aperture containment contract.

### 4. Round morphology

`round` now has a semantic guarantee: at full opening its aperture is circular within the sampling tolerance.

`ellipse` was added as the explicit shape when an elliptical aperture is actually wanted.

This prevents a parameter named `round` from silently producing an elongated ellipse.

### 5. Upper/lower lashes

The default relationship was corrected:

- upper lashes: short, heavier and denser;
- lower lashes: much shorter, lighter and sparser.

Long/tapered/clustered/disheveled forms remain available explicitly.
