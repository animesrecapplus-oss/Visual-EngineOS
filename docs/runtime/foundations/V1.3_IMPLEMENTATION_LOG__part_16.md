### 2026-09-18 — MOR canthus reconstruction micro-brick

#### Objective

Correct the concrete visual defect identified in the CanthusMetrics evidence: the legacy canthus
endpoint policy imposed a fixed `0.2 * half_height` floor even for `roundness = 0`, producing the
small vertical/triangular cap visible at the inner and outer corners of the generated anime-style
eyes. This step deliberately changes only endpoint reconstruction; it does not pretend to solve the
full canthus curvature family.

#### Implementation

- `src/visual_engine/components/face/eyes/geometry/canthus.py` — **L1-L68**. Replaced the fixed
  `0.2 * half_height` floor with the explicit reconstruction equation
  `R=min(0.35*half_height,max(half_width*roundness,0.02*min(half_width,half_height)))`; added
  finite/range validation and comments explaining the numerical floor. Inner and outer resolution
  remains independent.
- `tests/unit/test_v14_mor_canthus_reconstruction.py` — **L1-L107**. Added endpoint-scale,
  independence and dense direct-geometry tests. The main challenge matrix exercises 6480 combinations
  across 9 shapes, 3 openings, 4 resolutions, 3 rotations, 4 gaze directions and 5 asymmetric
  inner/outer roundness pairs. Full-builder tests separately preserve continuity/self-intersection
  checks. An extreme contour-variation case is expected to remain rejected; the reconstruction must
  not hide an unrelated invalid contour.
- `tools/visual_qa_v14_mor_canthus_reconstruction.py` — **L1-L159**. Generates actual before/after
  eye SVG/PNG renders for 9 reference-family-inspired cases, 33-point sampled bridge overlays and
  magnified corner diagnostics. Timing is kept out of the deterministic manifest.
- `tools/verify_v14_mor_canthus_reconstruction.py` — **L1-L49**. Fail-closed gate for implementation
  budget, promoted atoms, focused tests, visual QA and repeated-manifest determinism.
- `docs/components/eyes/morphology/V1.4_MOR_CANTHUS_RECONSTRUCTION_CONTRACT.md` — **L1-L78**. Mathematical contract, empirical
  floor-selection procedure, invariants, visual-evidence policy and explicit nonclaims.
- `docs/components/eyes/blueprint/V1.4_EYE_MODULE_OWNERSHIP.md` — appended ownership rule for endpoint reconstruction.
- `artifacts/A5_CURRENT_BASELINE_MANIFEST.json` — **L1-L9**. Updated the current A5 baseline to the deterministic post-reconstruction 88-file manifest while retaining the historical V1.3 manifest hash as provenance.
- `docs/components/eyes/blueprint/index.json` — implementation notes added to `MOR.canthus_inner` and
  `MOR.canthus_outer`; no new artificial atom was promoted.
- `docs/governance/roadmap/ROADMAP__part_01.md` — appended the reconstruction checkpoint and matrix dimensions.
- `docs/governance/decisions/DECISIONS__part_01.md` — appended **D-071**.

#### Challenge/correction history

The first candidate floor was swept coarsely. Floors below `0.02 * min(width,height)` failed on
low-resolution/low-opening/strong-gaze combinations in the current contour topology; `0.02` passed
the complete 6480-case direct geometry matrix. This is treated as an empirical robustness boundary,
not as a universal optimum. The generated before/after contact sheet was inspected separately from
the sampled diagnostic overlays: the after render removes the large triangular/cap-like corner
termination without moving the iris/pupil assembly. The sampled zoom is diagnostic only and is not
used as the final visual artifact.

#### Performance overlay

The new endpoint reconstruction adds no new integration or iterative solver. In the 9-case visual
campaign the mean geometry-build timing was approximately **9.55 ms after** versus **9.43 ms before**;
the difference is within normal run-to-run noise and is not claimed as an optimization. PNG raster
means were approximately **31.26 ms after** versus **31.38 ms before**. The timings are stored separately
in `artifacts/v14_mor_canthus_reconstruction/performance_timing.json` and are not part of deterministic
artifact identity.
