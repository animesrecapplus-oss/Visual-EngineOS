# V1.4 — Eye module ownership and anti-monolith audit

## Purpose

The eye is the reference component. Its implementation must remain inspectable while the domain is deepened. This audit makes file structure an explicit engineering invariant.

## Audit result

The previous eye domain had a 651-line `definitions.py` and a 425-line `geometry_builder.py`. Neither was yet a 5,000-line file, but both were clear future accumulation points: independent contracts, solvers and geometry responsibilities were co-located. The correct response is early decomposition, not waiting for a larger failure.

## Refactoring performed

`definitions.py` is now a compatibility facade. Its former responsibilities are owned by:

- `contracts/types.py`, `contracts/validation.py`
- `morphology/spec.py`
- `pupil/effects.py`, `pupil/spec.py`
- `iris/spec.py`
- `appearance/contour.py`, `appearance/spec.py`
- `eyelid/spec.py`
- `lashes/spec.py`
- `eyebrow/spec.py`
- `highlights/spec.py`
- `gaze/solver.py`
- `geometry/types.py`

`geometry_builder.py` is now a compatibility facade. Geometry construction is owned by:

- `geometry/shape.py`
- `geometry/curve.py`
- `geometry/contour.py`
- `geometry/containment.py`
- `geometry/lid_follow.py`
- `geometry/builder.py`

## Non-goal

This change is structural. It does **not** claim that the V1.4 morphology, iris microstructure, expression intelligence, colour intelligence, prompt compiler, camera model, or artistic rendering work is complete. Those remain governed by the atomic blueprint and its evidence lifecycle.

## Acceptance invariant

Focused eye implementation modules must remain at or below 250 lines unless a future architectural decision explicitly changes the budget. Compatibility facades are exempt only because they contain no implementation.

## Functional ownership rule added by MOR canthus brick

Canthus endpoint semantics now have one canonical owner: `geometry/canthus.py`. Contour construction consumes that contract and must not recreate inner/outer corner formulas locally. Contour safety invariants belong to `geometry/invariants.py`, not to the renderer. This prevents future canthus, eyelid and style work from duplicating corner logic in multiple modules.

## Functional ownership rule added by MOR.continuity

`geometry/continuity.py` is the sole owner of the mathematical contour-continuity
contract: endpoint tangent convention, cubic canthus bridges, continuity report and
deterministic bridge sampling. `geometry/invariants.py` remains the owner of generic
finite/self-intersection checks. The SVG renderer serializes the bridge controls and
must not recompute canthus geometry. This separation prevents a future renderer fix
from silently changing the geometric contract.


## Functional ownership added by MOR orthogonality

`morphology/orthogonality.py` owns the declared parameter→geometry-channel dependency
map and executable contamination report. Geometry construction must use stable nominal
reference frames for internal ocular offsets; sampled contour bounds must not silently
move the iris/pupil when only contour variation changes.

## Functional ownership added by granular visibility

`visibility.py` owns the hierarchical eye-granularity visibility tree. Rendering consumes
its resolved booleans; visibility does not enter geometry identity. The prompt compiler may
compile into this contract later, but no renderer or morphology module may invent prompt
inheritance rules locally.


## Functional ownership added by MOR envelope metrics

`morphology/envelope.py` is the sole owner of the renderer-neutral numerical measurement
layer for the resolved ocular envelope. It consumes final upper/lower lid samples and
semantic canthi; it does not generate, repair, or serialize geometry. The local frame,
projected height, aspect ratio, paired-lid opening profile, exact half-interval opening
integrals, opening balance and apex coordinates therefore have one canonical owner.
No renderer or geometry builder may duplicate these measurements locally.

## Functional ownership added by MOR CanthusMetrics

`morphology/canthus_metrics.py` is the sole owner of renderer-neutral measurements for a resolved
cubic canthus bridge: chord/arc length, local tangent angles, total/mean/peak curvature, turning
simplicity, curvature concentration, chord deviation and the explicitly diagnostic triangular-
closure evidence index. It consumes `geometry/continuity.py` bridge controls and never regenerates
or repairs them. `geometry/continuity.py` remains the owner of C1 construction/attachment. No
renderer is permitted to invent a second canthus measurement formula.


## Functional ownership added by MOR canthus reconstruction micro-brick

`geometry/canthus.py` remains the single owner of inner/outer endpoint reconstruction. The
resolved half-gap is derived from the corresponding roundness plus a documented numerical
robustness floor; no renderer duplicates the formula. The bridge itself remains owned by
`geometry/continuity.py`, so endpoint semantics and continuity construction cannot silently diverge.


## V1.50 pilot ownership correction

`LashShadowSpec` is owned by `lashes/effects.py`, not `pupil/effects.py`. Existing public/compatibility exports may re-export it, but the implementation owner is unique. This correction was discovered by the `CMP-EYE-PUPIL` component-truth pilot.
