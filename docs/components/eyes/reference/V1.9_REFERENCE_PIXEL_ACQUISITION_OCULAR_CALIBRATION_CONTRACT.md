# V1.9 — Reference Pixel Acquisition + Digitized Ocular Calibration

**Status:** `IMPLEMENTED — ENGINEERING PASS / PIXEL SOURCE GATE OPEN`

## Scope

V1.9 has two responsibilities:

1. correct the rigid binocular/closed-eye transform behavior exposed by the V1.8 visual inspection;
2. provide the complete fail-closed acquisition, segmentation and measurement machinery for reference-pixel calibration.

The second responsibility is intentionally source-gated. The engine does **not** convert a filename, a bounding box, a web preview, or an old envelope table into pixel truth.

## Transform contract

A pair rotation is a parent-level rigid transform.

For left/right eye anchors `L` and `R`, the binocular midpoint `M` is preserved and the centerline is rotated by the common pair axis angle:

`L' = rotate(L, M, theta)`

`R' = rotate(R, M, theta)`

The eye-local geometry then inherits its own `rotation_deg`. In the human-default pair the two values are normally identical; for the permitted small asymmetry, the pair axis uses their arithmetic mean.

This means that at 0/±30/±45/90 degrees:

- the two eyes remain parallel;
- the line joining their centers rotates with them;
- the midpoint is invariant;
- the left/right medial corners continue to face the pair center;
- brow placement follows the corresponding eye;
- gaze remains an internal ocular behavior rather than a rigid envelope rotation.

## Closed-eye contract

A closed eye is not a special horizontal world-space line.

The lid closure is first represented in the eye's canonical local frame and only then transformed by the parent/eye rotation. Therefore the closed lid, brow and all inherited ocular children share the same parent axis.

## Gaze-frame contract

Explicit scene targets are inverse-transformed into the eye-local frame before `GazeSolver` resolves the aperture. Named directions remain semantic eye-local directions.

The solved iris/pupil assembly is then transformed back through the eye rotation. This keeps:

`gaze != envelope rotation`

while preserving parent inheritance for the internal assembly.

## Reference acquisition

`reference/acquisition.py` provides:

- SHA-256 inspection;
- image dimension inspection;
- exact source verification;
- temporary download + verification before promotion;
- fail-closed rejection on hash/dimension mismatch;
- resolution-scaled foreground segmentation proposals;
- explicit candidate-box status rather than automatic truth.

`V19_REFERENCE_SOURCES.json` records two known source identities.

The first source (`53267426...`) has a web candidate located from the asset id, but the candidate is not admitted until its pixels match the historical 1176×980 and SHA-256 contract. The second source (`il_fullxfull.6486847481_s7a4.webp`) remains unresolved from the available project/checkpoint data.

## Segmentation contract

Segmentation does not assume a fixed number of rows or columns. It estimates the background from image borders, extracts foreground by color distance, performs resolution-scaled morphology and returns candidate boxes.

Candidate boxes have status:

`segmentation_candidates_require_visual_review`

They are not automatically converted to `ReferenceSample`.

## Digitization contract

Once an exact source is admitted, a sample may contain:

- ordered upper curve;
- ordered lower curve;
- medial terminal;
- lateral terminal;
- caruncle polygon;
- caruncle color/opacity when measurable;
- epicanthic curve only when visibly supported.

The four endpoint axes are independent:

- inner-upper;
- inner-lower;
- outer-upper;
- outer-lower.

`trace.py` provides descriptive measurements for arc length, endpoint tangent, discrete curvature and an explicitly parameterized tangent-stability transition length.

The transition threshold is recorded in the output and is **not** treated as anatomy.

## Three-family contract

Reference family labels remain descriptive:

- `human_generic`;
- `anime_stylized`;
- `exaggerated_artistic`.

`unclassified` remains valid until visible evidence supports a family assignment.

## Caruncle / epicanthus contract

The caruncle and epicanthic fold remain separate fields. Missing pixels are not encoded as zero.

`UNKNOWN / missing` means that the source does not currently provide enough evidence.

## Comparison contract

Reference-vs-engine comparison is only emitted after exact source admission and actual digitization. The comparison domains are:

- envelope;
- upper contour;
- lower contour;
- four endpoint tangents;
- terminals;
- caruncle;
- epicanthus.

No style-band fit is promoted from envelope-only evidence.

## Visual QA gate

V1.9 generates real engine renders and sampled transform diagnostics for:

- pair 0/±30/±45/90 degrees;
- closed-eye −45/0/30/45/90 degrees.

The sampled pair diagnostic marks actual centers and actual orientation vectors. The sampled closed-eye diagnostic marks actual terminal points.

No reference overlay is generated while exact reference pixels are unavailable.

## Non-goals

V1.9 does not:

- invent control points from bounding boxes;
- call a web preview the original source without hash/dimension verification;
- fit universal anime anatomy from one sheet;
- infer epicanthic geometry from unrelated facial marks;
- replace the runtime world-transform resolver;
- make gaze rotate the eyelid envelope.
