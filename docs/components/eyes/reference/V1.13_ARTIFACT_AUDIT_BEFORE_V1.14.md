## Confirmed useful evidence

### V1.10/V1.11 reference evidence

The review-board and crop-montage images are useful as human-review evidence only. The V1.11 manifest remains explicitly pending; no candidate was approved by the engine. These files therefore must not be treated as calibrated eye samples or MOR observations.

### V1.13 spherical evidence

The V1.13 contact sheet and sampled renders are useful for verifying that the engine emitted spherical projection geometry and that the sampled diagnostics landed on the stored projected iris geometry.

They also exposed a concrete implementation defect during visual inspection:

- at yaw 30° and 60°, the iris was projected near the lateral side of the aperture;
- the pupil was displaced farther laterally instead of remaining attached to the iris;
- the measured V1.13 coordinates confirmed that the pupil surface center was being generated at a radial distance unrelated to the globe radius.

For the original V1.13 implementation:

```text
iris surface center at yaw 30°  = (0.2200, 0.0000, 0.3811)
pupil surface center at yaw 30° = (0.4444, 0.0000, 0.7697)
```

The globe radius was `0.5`, so the pupil point was not a child of the iris surface. The same failure increased at yaw 60° and 89.9°.

This is a confirmed geometry defect, not a subjective interpretation.

## V1.13 visual conclusions retained for V1.14

1. A spherical parent must own the iris surface attachment.
2. A pupil must be resolved from the iris attachment frame, never from the eye origin after the iris has moved.
3. A projected child must inherit the parent's translation and tangent frame.
4. The eyelid aperture must remain a separate morphology owner.
5. 2D clipping is still valid as the final SVG aperture mask, but V1.14 must also retain 3D lid depth metadata.
6. A mathematically valid sphere projection is insufficient evidence if a child visibly detaches from its parent.
7. V1.11 reference artifacts are review evidence only and remain human-approval gated.

## Cleanup decision

After this document was created and the relevant images/manifests were inspected, the old generated artifact directories were removed. V1.14 generates a fresh artifact directory; no V1.10/V1.11/V1.13 generated artifacts are carried forward merely for volume.
