# Iris relations — V1.84

`REL-IRIS-PARENT` — Iris belongs to the eyeball branch of the ocular hierarchy. The aggregate/public eye parent is
`CMP-EYE`; no standalone executable eyeball owner is invented.

`REL-IRIS-PUPIL` — Iris → Pupil is the direct semantic child relation. It is explicitly grounded by V1.15/V1.17 and
formalized by V1.83. Pupil containment remains a downstream geometry contract.

`REL-IRIS-EYELID` — iris exposure/occlusion is related to the face-owned eyelid surface; no local Iris owner is
claimed for eyelid geometry.

`REL-IRIS-GAZE` — gaze resolves the ocular assembly; no local Iris gaze solver is claimed.

`REL-IRIS-CORNEA` — cornea is a sibling ocular surface, not an Iris child in this dossier.

`REL-IRIS-HIGHLIGHT` — highlights/reflections are a cross-relation system, not a fixed Iris child.

`REL-IRIS-CACHE` — only iris shape currently participates in the verified eye geometry cache projection; V1.84 does
not change this boundary.
