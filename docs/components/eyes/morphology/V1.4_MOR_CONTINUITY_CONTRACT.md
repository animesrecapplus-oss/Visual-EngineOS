# V1.4 MOR.continuity — Geometry Contract

## Scope

`MOR.continuity` closes only the contour-integrity continuity atom. It does not
close `MOR.morph_orthogonality` or `MOR.morph_variation`.

The contract applies to the open-eye aperture (`opening > 0`). The `closed` and
`covered` states remain an explicit degenerate boundary representation and do not
claim a non-degenerate C1 contour.

## Mathematical definition

The eye aperture is traversed as:

`upper(inner → outer) → outer bridge → lower(outer → inner) → inner bridge → upper`.

Each sampled lid curve is serialized by the renderer's cubic interpolation rule.
At an endpoint, that rule has the derivative convention:

- upper start: `T = (P2 - P0) / 2`;
- upper end: `T = (Pn-1 - Pn-3) / 2`;
- the same convention is applied to the reversed lower curve.

A canthus bridge is a cubic Bézier `B(P0,P1,P2,P3)` whose derivative is
`3(P1-P0)` at its start and `3(P3-P2)` at its end. Therefore the bridge is built
with:

`P1 = P0 + T_start / 3`

`P2 = P3 - T_end / 3`.

This makes the bridge derivative vector equal to the adjacent lid derivative,
not merely collinear. The join is therefore C1 under the declared interpolation
convention. C2 continuity is deliberately not claimed.

## Invariants

1. **Finiteness** — all lid and bridge control points are finite.
2. **Exact attachment** — every bridge endpoint equals its semantic canthus/lid
   endpoint.
3. **C1 equality** — bridge endpoint derivatives equal the adjacent lid endpoint
   derivatives within the scale-aware numerical tolerance.
4. **Non-degenerate tangent** — an open contour must have a non-zero endpoint
   tangent at every canthus join.
5. **Topology safety** — deterministic bridge samples must not introduce a
   non-adjacent contour crossing.
6. **Post-gaze validation** — the same continuity contract is evaluated after
   gaze-follow deformation; validating only the pre-gaze contour is insufficient.
7. **Opening boundary** — positive openings remain non-degenerate down to the
   tested numerical boundary; `opening == 0` is an explicit degenerate state and
   is not falsely reported as C1.
8. **Scale-aware numerical policy** — contour crossing tolerance is relative to
   geometry scale. A fixed absolute epsilon is not used as the default because it
   creates false positives when the aperture becomes very small.
9. **Renderer neutrality** — bridge construction and validation live in geometry;
   the renderer only serializes the already-resolved cubic controls.

## What is not claimed

- biological/anatomical correctness of the canthus;
- C2 or curvature continuity;
- perspective/3D continuity;
- continuity of all future morphology parameters;
- global morph orthogonality;
- global parameter-manifold variation continuity;
- visual equivalence merely from matching numerical continuity metrics.

## Evidence required for acceptance

The atom is accepted only after:

`DEVELOP → TEST → ARTIFACT → VISUALIZE → CORRECT → VALIDATE → ACCEPT`.

Evidence must include nominal, boundary, adversarial, resolution, rotation and
post-gaze cases, plus deterministic SVG/PNG/manifest output. The continuity
contract must be tested independently of the renderer and then exercised through
the renderer as a serialization consumer.
